## What I build

**Design + evaluation frameworks for AI-generated instruction**
[GUIDE](https://github.com/jermn007/GUIDE) (Grounded Universal Instructional Design Evaluator) is a learning-science framework that both **designs and grades** instruction. One set of 60 theory-grounded dimensions across 10 ADDIE archetypes, run two directions: forward to author instructional content, backward to evaluate it. Scoring uses the LLM-as-a-judge pattern; every criterion is anchored to named research - Knowles, Mayer, Sweller, Gagne, Bloom, Vygotsky, and others. Ships as a Claude skill + plugin with a 13-agent ADDIE pipeline. Built as a capstone synthesis of my UCF MA in Instructional Systems.

**AI-powered learning tools**
RAG-enabled assistants trained on curriculum materials, grounded in retrieval-augmented generation to reduce hallucinations and support evidence-informed design. I co-authored research on this at the 2025 AECT Annual Convention and delivered a live build-along webinar for AECT's Education in Health Professions SIG (March 2026).

**Learning technology infrastructure**
I've designed and administered enterprise learning ecosystems spanning LCMS, LXP, microlearning platforms, digital adoption platforms (WalkMe), internal LLMs, and xAPI infrastructure - driving a 600% increase in xAPI verb generation that gave content teams actionable analytics on learner engagement for the first time.

**Python tools for learning workflows**
My repos reflect real problems I've encountered: automating systematic literature review workflows, monitoring SaaS platform health through Home Assistant sensors, and converting game audio formats for VR learning environments.
